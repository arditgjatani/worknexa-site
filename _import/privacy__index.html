Privacy

Worknexa collects the information you provide in the launch-interest forms. Contact: contact@worknexa.eu.

We use your contact details, company needs or professional information only to register your interest and contact you about the Worknexa launch. Registration is not a job application, job offer or recruitment service agreement.

We keep launch-interest data for 12 months from registration, unless you request earlier deletion. After 12 months it is deleted.

You can withdraw consent or request access, correction or deletion of your data by emailing contact@worknexa.eu.

Data is stored on Cloudflare infrastructure through Sites. Access in the application is limited to the authorised administrator. WhatsApp is optional and subject to its own privacy terms. Unfinished form data is stored only in your browser session when switching language. No documents or CVs are requested.
