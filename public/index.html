Current vacancy – Gjatani Construction (direct employer)

Steel fixers (rebar tying) – factory in Brežice, Slovenia.

€7 net per hour, about €1,200–1,350 net per month.

Direct employer: Gjatani Construction. 5 positions, 1-year contract.

Full time, 40 hours per week; overtime within Slovenian legal limits.

Accommodation €150 per month; water and electricity paid by the company. Company car and fuel provided; work uniform provided.

Social insurance and taxes paid by the company.

Little experience needed; on-site training; basic English required.

Visa and work permit handled by the employer, about 2 months.

Possible move to Belgium after at least one month and the A1 document: €12 net per hour plus 13th salary, accommodation €230 per month; not guaranteed, depends on documents and projects.

This is Gjatani Construction’s own vacancy, not a Worknexa placement service. Use the candidate register-interest form below to express interest in this vacancy. Worknexa’s recruitment services have not launched yet.

Clear answers before we begin.

How can an agency in a source country partner with Worknexa?

Agencies can register interest now using the employer form, stating sourcing markets in the message. After launch, we will discuss capacity and partnership criteria. Each engagement will require a written agreement.

Does Worknexa guarantee employment or a visa?

No. Registration does not guarantee employment, a project or a visa. Future opportunities will depend on employer requirements, candidate eligibility and the relevant authorities. The vacancy shown is Gjatani Construction’s own direct-employer listing.

How are services priced?

After launch, each proposal will be prepared individually. Recruitment fees and additional costs will be specified separately in an agreement before services begin.

Which European destinations can I enquire about?

You can register interest in future European opportunities. After launch, employer requirements and employment eligibility will be assessed case by case.

Can a company enquire about projects?

Yes. Companies and contractors can use the employer interest form and describe their services and target markets in the message. We will contact you about the launch and future collaboration.
